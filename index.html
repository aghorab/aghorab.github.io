Become a tester

Play my games before anyone else.

I am looking for people to try new games and apps before they reach Google Play: play a build for a few days, then tell me what felt confusing, too hard or broken. Testers get every new game first, and their name in the credits if they like.

  • An Android phone or tablet is all you need.
  • Google Play invites testers by the Google account on their phone, so the form asks for it.
  • No obligation: test when you have time, leave whenever you want.
