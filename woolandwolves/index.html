Wool & Wolves

One cut. One clever plan. Save the flock.

Wolves and sheep hang from ropes over a ravine. Cut the right ropes and every wolf falls, while the flock stays safe. Two hundred puzzles across ten worlds, and it plays offline.

How to play

Swipe to cut. One swipe is one cut, even when it crosses several ropes.
Wolves down, sheep safe. Every wolf must end in the ravine. No sheep may fall or be touched by a wolf.
Beat the par. Find the plan with the fewest cuts for three stars. No timer and no lives.
Hints in three steps. Where to look, which rule matters, then the rope itself. And when it works, watch your own plan play back.
