Privacy Policy: Wool & Wolves

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Wool & Wolves (the "App"), published by Ahmed Ghorab, Hany Meličkovej 2984/19A, 841 05 Bratislava, Karlova Ves, Slovakia (the "Publisher").

1. Data we collect

The App has no accounts and no registration, and it does not collect your name, email address or contacts. Every puzzle is built into the App, and your progress, stars, badges and settings are stored only on your device. The Publisher runs no server and receives none of this data.

2. Advertising

The App shows advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited): a banner at the bottom of the menu screens, a full screen advertisement that may appear between levels, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch in return for a hint or to open a level early. No advertisement is shown while a puzzle is on the screen. To serve and measure these advertisements, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the App may collect and process device data such as the advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and interactions with the advertisements. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and Google explains how it uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message when the App first starts, and may choose between personalised and non-personalised advertising. You can change that choice at any time under Settings, Advertising choices.

3. Reminders and sharing

Reminders are off until you turn them on in Settings. They are scheduled on your device and are not sent from any server. Sharing a result or the App happens only when you press Share, through your device's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where you give consent, personalised advertising is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Non-personalised advertising and the basic operation of the App rely on legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can reset or delete your device advertising identifier at any time in your device settings. Uninstalling the App removes all progress stored on the device. You may lodge a complaint with the Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
