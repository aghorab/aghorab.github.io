// Shikaku logic puzzle · Android

Rectango 四角

Divide the grid into rectangles. One answer, pure logic.

Rectango is Shikaku, the Japanese rectangle puzzle, made calm and clear for your phone. Every rectangle holds exactly one number, and its area equals that number. Cover the whole grid, with no overlaps and no gaps.

What's in the game · version 1.19

  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and it can be reached by logic alone. No guessing.
  • Four sizes: 5×5, 7×7, 9×9 and 11×11; the big grids open as you play.
  • Difficulty rises with you, so the puzzles keep up as you get better.
  • A daily, a weekly and a monthly challenge, the same board for everyone, with a daily streak to keep.
  • Five colour themes: Classic, Sakura, Lagoon, Meadow and Night.
  • Pencil dots: long-press a cell to mark it while you think.
  • Expert mode: no colours until the board is full.
  • Hints that point at your mistake, not just the next piece.
  • Best times for every size and difficulty, a stats screen and 14 goals to reach.
  • Playable with a keyboard and a screen reader, not only by touch.
  • Plays offline, in English, Slovak, Arabic, French, Spanish and German.

Quiet by design: ink on paper, soft sounds, optional calm music, nothing flashing at you.

Privacy

No account and no sign in. Your puzzles and progress stay on your phone.
