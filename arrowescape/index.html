Arrow Escape

The board is full of arrows. Only one of them can leave. Find the arrow with a clear way out, release it, and watch the space it leaves open the way for the next.

A puzzle, not a grind

No undo and no score. Three lives, a time limit, and three stars: for finishing, for finishing quickly and for finishing without a crash. Tapping the same blocked arrow again is free, so trying an idea costs nothing.

Something new every day

A daily board, one attempt. Six animal boards every week under a different rule. A board of roughly two hundred arrows every month. Fifteen secret boards, unlocked by how you play rather than how far you get. Badges, and a trophy room with one trophy for each world.

Made to be pleasant

Six board themes including two dark ones, nine arrow styles, a palette for colour blind players, larger arrowheads, a left handed layout, reduced motion and three text sizes. Gentle music written by the game itself.

Plays anywhere

Every board is in the game already. No account, no sign in, and it works in aeroplane mode. Your progress stays on your phone.

How to play

Tap to release. An arrow flies along its path and leaves at the outer frame, if nothing is in the way.
Mind the crash. A blocked arrow costs one of three lives. Tapping the same blocked arrow again is free.
Beat the clock. Every board has a time limit. Earn stars for finishing, for time left, and for no crashes.
Look first. There is no undo. Usually only one arrow can escape at the start, and each release opens the next path.
